# Seven-Day Continuity Audit

## Simulation

Engine: `buildInnerworkDailyDecision()`.

Issue: Over Responsibility. Reflections were mixed; Day 4 was skipped.

| Day | Main practice | Mode | Duration |
|---:|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Body Awareness | Reflection | 4 |
| 2 | Journaling | Reflection | 7 |
| 3 | Body Awareness | Reflection | 4 |
| 4 | Journaling | Reflection | 7 |
| 5 | Body Awareness | Recovery | 2 |
| 6 | Journaling | Reflection | 7 |
| 7 | Body Awareness | Reflection | 4 |

The six support practices were identical on all seven days.

## What changes

- Main practice alternates through date hashing and recent-ID avoidance.
- A skip changes the next day to Recovery and shortens duration.
- Mixed helpful/heavy reflections produce scoring signals.

## What does not change

- Issue remains fixed.
- Support inventory remains fixed.
- No cumulative level or stage is stored.
- No automatic escalation to Growth occurs from repeated helpful outcomes.

## Verdict

Seven-day continuity is reactive and short-memory-based, not progressive.
