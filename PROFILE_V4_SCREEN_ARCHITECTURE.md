# PROFILE V4 - SCREEN ARCHITECTURE

## OVERVIEW
This document translates the `PROFILE_V4_FINAL_IDENTITY_WAREHOUSE` into a concrete mobile user experience. It dictates information hierarchy, screen flows, progressive disclosure rules, and monetization strategy.

The guiding principle for the Profile V4 UI is **Progressive Depth**. Users should never be overwhelmed by long walls of text. Information must unfold intuitively—starting from simple identity validation and descending into deep shadow work and daily action.

---

## SECTION 1: INFORMATION HIERARCHY

The 8 sections are ranked to ensure cognitive ease and retention.

- **Most Important (Above the fold)**
  - Fase Kehidupan Saat Ini (State & Timing)
  - Siapa Dirimu (Identity)
- **Important (Primary scrolling view)**
  - Energi & Mekanika (Operations)
  - Luka, Bayangan & Warisan (Healing)
- **Optional (Secondary scrolling view)**
  - Karya & Talenta (Contribution)
  - Cinta & Relasi (Intimacy)
- **Advanced (Bottom of screen)**
  - Raga & Ruang (Health & Environment)
- **Hidden (Accessible via deep links or specific triggers)**
  - Spiritualitas & Evolusi (Spiritual tendencies/Clairs)

## SECTION 2: FIRST SCREEN EXPERIENCE

When a user taps the "Profile" tab, the initial view must immediately ground them in *today*.

**Visible Without Scrolling:**
1. **Header**: Name, Profile Image, and the **Arketipe Utama** as a subtitle (e.g., "Aria - Sang Pembimbing yang Tenang").
2. **Kondisimu Saat Ini**: A minimalist dynamic state-logger (e.g., "Energi Sedang Rendah").
3. **Fokus Hari Ini**: One single actionable task.
4. **Misi Kehidupan**: A highly aesthetic, un-collapsible manifesto block at the top.

**Collapsed (Below the fold):**
- All other sections (Energi, Peta Jiwa, Talenta) are collapsed by default into sleek, tappable horizontal or vertical accordions to prevent visual clutter.

**Expanded (Below the fold):**
- Only the "Fase Kehidupan Saat Ini" (Current Chapter) is partially expanded to contextualize the user's current month/semester.

## SECTION 6: FREE VS PREMIUM

Monetization must rely on **Depth** rather than **Withholding**. Free users must still feel they are receiving immense value, while Premium users unlock actionable depth.

### FREE (The "What")
- **Remains Visible**: 
  - Arketipe Utama
  - Misi Kehidupan
  - Otoritas Batin & Strategi Aksi
  - Fokus Hari Ini & Kondisimu Saat Ini
  - DNA Talenta (Top 3)
  - Kebutuhan Emosional
  - Aura Dominan (Visual only)
- **User Trust Impact**: Extremely high. The user immediately learns their purpose, decision-making style, and daily focus.

### PREMIUM (The "How" & "Why")
- **Locked/Teaser**:
  - Semester 1 & Semester 2 Breakdowns (Timing/Future)
  - Money Block & Love Block (Deep Trauma)
  - Pola Sabotase & Warisan Leluhur (Shadow Work)
  - Cara Tubuhmu Bekerja (Advanced physiological secrets)
  - Clair Potential & Jejak Jiwa (Esoteric deep dives)
  - Aliran Rezeki (Wealth mechanics)
- **Conversion Strategy**: Tease the Premium cards with the *Title* and a *Blurred one-liner* (e.g., "Love Block: Kamu cenderung menarik pasangan yang... [Buka dengan Premium]").

## SECTION 7: MOBILE UX

### Card Size & Layout
- **Dynamic Cards** (State, Focus): Small, pill-shaped or compact rectangles to save vertical space.
- **Identity Cards** (Archetype, Purpose): Large, full-width "Hero" cards with rich background gradients.
- **Detail Cards** (Shadow, Talents): Uniform grid (2x2) of square or rectangular buttons that open into modals or drawers.

### Expand Behavior (Progressive Disclosure)
- Clicking a card does **not** navigate to a new page.
- It opens a **Bottom Sheet (Drawer)** that covers 80% of the screen.
- This maintains the user's context of being in their "Identity Warehouse".

### Long Text Handling
- **No Walls of Text**: Text exceeding 3 paragraphs must be broken up.
- **Format**: Use bullet points, bold keywords, and the "Bite, Snack, Meal" approach.
  - *Bite*: The title and 1-sentence summary on the card face.
  - *Snack*: The first paragraph in the expanded Bottom Sheet.
  - *Meal*: A "Baca Selengkapnya" (Read More) or secondary tab inside the Bottom Sheet for the deep esoteric breakdown.

### Insight Handling
- Every expanded card must end with an actionable "Saran Praktis" (Practical Advice) block.
- Action blocks must have a button to "Tambahkan ke Jurnal" (Add to Journal) or "Diskusikan dengan Gaia" (Discuss with Gaia).
