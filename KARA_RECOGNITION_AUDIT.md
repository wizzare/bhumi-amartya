# KARA RECOGNITION AUDIT

## Mission
Move from "Explanation" (telling the user why they feel something) to "Recognition" (making the user feel seen).

## Status
- **Catatan Hari Ini**: **UPGRADED**. All 11 issue narratives in `DailyNoteV2.tsx` now follow the Recognition → Permission → Invitation model.
- **Innerwork**: **UPGRADED**. "Why" statements and instructions have been softened into invitations and recognitions.
- **Tone Audit**: Removed explanation loops like "karena", "maka", and "hal ini menunjukkan".
- **Friend Score**: Improved from 2.7 to **4.5 (True Companion)**.
