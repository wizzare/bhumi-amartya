## Opening Variety

Each section begins from a different source:

- History and state
- Emotional/cognitive condition
- Money and work pattern
- Love and attachment pattern
- Relationship and boundaries
- Purpose and journey
- Shadow and current pressure
- Talents and momentum

No shared global atmosphere paragraph is prepended to every section.

## Saran Bhumi Variety

Every advice begins with a domain-specific orientation:

- Mental: organizing thought
- Keuangan: resource stability
- Percintaan: emotional need
- Relasi: boundaries and support
- Spiritual: allowing meaning
- Tantangan: recognizing automatic patterns
- Peluang: using existing strengths
