## Safety

Human Meaning is used rather than raw canonical fields in displayed prose.

The UI does not intentionally expose:

- Arcana numbers
- Gates or channels
- Chakra scores
- Raw numerology
- Raw astrology
- Internal engine names

## Personalization Finding

Sections can no longer be swapped freely:

- Mental describes cognitive and emotional pressure.
- Keuangan describes resource security and decision style.
- Percintaan describes heart-level patterns.
- Relasi describes boundaries, belonging, and support.
- Spiritual describes purpose, growth, and meaning.
