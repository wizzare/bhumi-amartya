## Core Principles

### Reflection-first

Soul Identity language must invite reflection before conclusion. The user should feel guided to notice, contemplate, and embody meaning rather than accept a fixed label.

### Resonance, not absolute identity

Resonance describes a meaningful symbolic relationship. It must not be presented as literal origin, rank, destiny, or permanent identity.

### Symbolic interpretation

Soul Identity terms are symbolic mirrors. They help translate existing Blueprint and Human Meaning data into contemplative language.

### Multiple blueprint synthesis

Soul Identity may draw from the existing 8 Blueprint systems only through approved synthesis. No single Blueprint system should be treated as the sole truth of the soul.

### Human-centered language

All terminology must return to everyday human experience: choices, relationships, body, emotions, growth, contribution, boundaries, and self-understanding.

### Ethical interpretation

Soul Identity must avoid fear, superiority, spiritual inflation, diagnosis, fatalism, and shame. Every interpretation must preserve agency.

### No deterministic claims

No term may imply that the user is permanently fixed, cosmically superior, spiritually doomed, or obligated to fulfill a rigid mission.

## Official Terms

## General

### Soul Identity

Definition: The reflective identity layer that describes a user's symbolic mission, gifts, lessons, shadow, archetype, and resonance.

Purpose: To help the user recognize deeper patterns in their existing Blueprint and Human Meaning.

Scope: Knowledge and reflection layer only.

Related Terms: Soul Layer, Soul Identity Layer, Soul Archetype, Resonance.

Notes: Must not be presented as absolute truth.

### Soul Layer

Definition: A general term for the symbolic layer of meaning that sits above raw Blueprint data and below user-facing reflection.

Purpose: To describe the conceptual area where soul-level interpretation happens.

Scope: Documentation language only.

Related Terms: Soul Identity, Knowledge Layer, Reflection Layer.

Notes: Use `Soul Identity Layer` when referring to the formal system.

### Resonance

Definition: A meaningful symbolic relationship between the user and an archetype, mission, lesson, gift, Starseed pattern, or civilization pattern.

Purpose: To describe recognition without claiming literal fact.

Scope: Soul Identity interpretation only.

Related Terms: Resonance Pattern, Hybrid Resonance, Starseed Resonance, Civilization Resonance.

Notes: Use "resonates with" rather than "is".

### Resonance Pattern

Definition: A recurring symbolic theme that appears across the Soul Identity interpretation.

Purpose: To identify consistent thematic signals.

Scope: Knowledge and interpretation language only.

Related Terms: Resonance, Primary Resonance, Secondary Resonance.

Notes: Does not imply scoring or ranking by itself.

### Hybrid Resonance

Definition: A resonance expression where more than one symbolic family is meaningfully present.

Purpose: To acknowledge mixed or layered resonance without forcing one label.

Scope: Starseed and Civilization interpretation.

Related Terms: Primary Resonance, Secondary Resonance, Dominant Resonance.

Notes: Hybrid Resonance is not higher, rarer, or better.

### Soul Output

Definition: Any user-facing result produced from the Soul Identity System.

Purpose: To distinguish displayed meaning from internal knowledge libraries.

Scope: Runtime, documents, prompts, CMS, and QA language.

Related Terms: Profile Card, Reflection Layer, Soul Identity Section.

Notes: A Soul Output must follow ethical language rules.

### Identity Extension

Definition: An approved addition to the user's identity experience that extends existing Gudang Identitas Jiwa without replacing it.

Purpose: To describe Track G as an extension, not a redesign.

Scope: Product and runtime planning language.

Related Terms: Soul Identity Section, Gudang Identitas Jiwa.

Notes: Placement requires Founder approval.

### Knowledge Layer

Definition: The documentation and content foundation that defines meanings, terms, libraries, and interpretation boundaries.

Purpose: To separate knowledge preparation from implementation.

Scope: Source of Truth and glossary documents.

Related Terms: Soul Layer, Resonance Library, Archetype Library.

Notes: Knowledge Layer is not engine logic.

## Soul Identity

### Soul Archetype

Definition: A symbolic role or pattern that reflects how the user's purpose, energy, lessons, and gifts tend to move together.

Purpose: To help the user recognize a recurring inner role.

Scope: Soul Identity reflection.

Related Terms: Archetype Library, Soul Mission, Resonance Pattern.

Definition: A contribution theme that expresses what the user may be learning to embody or offer.

Related Terms: Mission Library, Soul Archetype, Soul Gift.

Definition: A natural capacity that can become beneficial when trained, embodied, and expressed with awareness.

### Soul Lesson

Definition: A recurring growth invitation that helps the user mature, integrate, or heal.

Purpose: To frame challenges without punishment or fatalism.

Scope: Soul Identity reflection.

Related Terms: Lessons Library, Soul Shadow, Growth.

Notes: A Soul Lesson is not a penalty.

### Soul Shadow

Definition: A protective or unconscious pattern that may distort the healthy expression of a Soul Gift, Soul Mission, or Soul Archetype.

Purpose: To name growth material compassionately.

Scope: Soul Identity reflection.

Related Terms: Soul Lesson, Shadow, Resonance Pattern.

Notes: Soul Shadow must never be framed as badness.
