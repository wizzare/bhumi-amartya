# INNERWORK NAVIGATOR RUNTIME

The Navigator determines the duration and intensity of the recommended practices based on the user's current capacity.

## Navigator Modes

### 1. RECOVERY
*   **Duration:** 2–5 minutes.
*   **Primary Goal:** Stabilization.
*   **Allowed:** Breathing, Grounding, Body awareness, Rest.
*   **Forbidden:** Heavy journaling, Long meditation, Intense workout.

### 2. REFLECTION
*   **Duration:** 5–10 minutes.
*   **Primary Goal:** Awareness.
*   **Allowed:** Reflection, Journaling, Light meditation.

### 3. GROWTH
*   **Duration:** 10–20 minutes.
*   **Primary Goal:** Action.
*   **Allowed:** Challenge, Habit building, Deep practice.

## Selection Logic
1.  **Issue First:** The practice must match the Dominant Issue from Catatan.
2.  **Capacity Filter:** The Navigator mode (Recovery/Reflection/Growth) filters the practice list for appropriate duration and intensity.
3.  **Wellness Override:** Physical state may further restrict allowed physical activities (Yoga/Activity).
