# INNERWORK COMPLETION-FIRST AUDIT

## The Shift: Recommendation Hub vs. Action Companion

| Feature | Old Hub (V5) | New Companion (V5.1) | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Content Strategy** | "Show everything relevant" | "Show only what is necessary" | Reduced Overwhelm |
| **Recovery View** | 10+ sections/options | 4 focused sections | Increased Completion |
| **Library** | Mixed into daily page | Segregated to "Zone B" tab | Cleaner UI |
| **Success Metric** | Click-through rate | Completion + Reflection | Value-driven |

## Validation: User Experience

### "I know what to do."
By presenting only one primary practice in RECOVERY and REFLECTION, the "What should I choose?" anxiety is eliminated. The user spends their energy on the **practice**, not the **selection**.

### "Bhumi is helping me."
The "Kenapa" section remains personal and human, ensuring the user understands the *why* without being buried in the *how many*.

## Final Conclusion
The V5.1 simplification effectively transforms Innerwork into a lean, effective coaching tool. By respecting the user's energy levels (Navigator), we maximize the chance that they will actually perform the healing work.
