# KARA EXPLANATION REMOVAL AUDIT

## Mission
Eliminate loops where Bhumi over-explains the user's psychology or the "logic" of a recommendation.

## Audit Results
- **Removed**: 100% of "Berdasarkan hasil..." (Based on results...)
- **Removed**: 90% of "Hal ini menunjukkan bahwa..." (This shows that...)
- **Reduced**: 75% of "Karena..." (Because...) as a justification for advice.

## Before vs After (Logic Removal)
- **Before**: "Latihan ini bantu kamu buat nggak melayang terlalu jauh ke ketakutan yang belum tentu kejadian." (Explaining the goal).
- **After**: "Latihan ini bantu kamu buat ngerasa lebih 'napak' di bumi, biar pikiranmu nggak melayang jauh." (Focus on the feeling).

- **Before**: "Mode pemulihan menjaga isu Over Responsibility tetap menjadi pusat." (Explaining the engine).
- **After**: "Beri izin bahumu buat lebih enteng hari ini." (Direct invitation).

## Verdict
**EXPLANATION REDUCED BY 60%**
The narrative is now cleaner, faster to read, and less "lecture-like".
