# INNERWORK V5 REALITY CHECK: Widhi Simulation

## Audit Results

### 1. Realism
Widhi would likely do the **Praktik Utama (2 minutes)** because the barrier to entry is extremely low. However, the presence of 10+ other items on the page might trigger "choice paralysis" or make her feel like she is failing by not doing everything.

### 2. Role Perception
*   **Top half (Fokus, Kenapa, Praktik Utama):** Feels like a **Life Coach**. It is empathetic, contextual, and direct.
*   **Bottom half (Mudra, Yoga, Activity, etc.):** Feels like an **Exercise Catalog**. It loses the "personal touch" and starts looking like a list of content to consume.

### 3. Scroll Behavior
A user in **RECOVERY** (Low Body/Moderate Emotion) is unlikely to scroll past the first button. If they do, they might feel overwhelmed by the sheer volume of "recommendations."

### 4. Post-Practice Reflection
The reflection is the strongest "Coach" element. It validates the user's struggle (especially for "Sama Saja" results), which is critical for someone dealing with guilt.

### 5. Final Verdict: PARTIAL
The core coaching (top) is excellent, but the structural requirement to show *every* category (Mudra, Yoga, etc.) creates a "homework" feeling that contradicts the "Recovery" goal.
