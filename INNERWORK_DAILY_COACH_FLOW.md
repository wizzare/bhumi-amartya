# INNERWORK DAILY COACH FLOW

This document outlines the 11-section structure of the Innerwork Daily Coach, ensuring a human-centric and actionable experience.

## Section 1: 🎯 Fokus Hari Ini
*   **Goal:** Short explanation in human language.
*   **Constraint:** Maximum 100 words.
*   **Content:** Answers "Why this matters today" based on the Dominant Issue.

## Section 2: 💡 Kenapa Bhumi Menyarankan Ini
*   **Content:** Explain the recommendation based on current state, wellness, navigator, and context.
*   **Rule:** **Never** mention Human Design, Numerology, Destiny Matrix, or Natal Chart. Use only human language.

## Section 3: 🌿 Praktik Utama
*   **Constraint:** Only **ONE** practice.
*   **Goal:** Highest impact.
*   **Requirement:** Must directly support the Dominant Issue.

## Section 4: 🌱 Praktik Pendukung
*   **Constraint:** Maximum 3 (Optional).
*   **Examples:** Journaling, Breathwork, Reflection, Gratitude, Emotional Processing.

## Section 5: 🎧 Audio yang Cocok Hari Ini
*   **Recommendations:** Audio Healing, Nature Healing, Sleep Healing, Emotional Healing.
*   **Logic:** Based on the specific issue.

## Section 6: 🧘 Meditasi
*   **Logic:** Issue-specific styles (e.g., Overthinking → Breath Awareness; People Pleasing → Boundary Meditation).
*   **Constraint:** Not generic.

## Section 7: 🙏 Mudra
*   **Requirement:** Recommend a specific mudra (e.g., Gyan, Apana, Prana).
*   **Details:** Must explain "Why" and the "Duration".

## Section 8: 🧎 Yoga
*   **Levels:** Beginner, Intermediate, Recovery.
*   **Details:** Must explain "Goal" and "Duration".

## Section 9: 🚶 Aktivitas Fisik
*   **Logic:** Based on wellness capacity.
*   **Constraint:** Never recommend heavy exercise to users in **RECOVERY** mode.

## Section 10: 🔎 Eksplorasi Lanjut
*   **Format:** Google and YouTube Search Suggestions (Phrases, not URLs).
*   **Example:** "5 minute body scan meditation".

## Section 11: 🌙 Besok Bhumi Akan Mengajakmu
*   **Goal:** Generate follow-up expectations to create continuity.
*   **Example:** If today is "Boundary Practice", tomorrow is "Observe emotional response".
