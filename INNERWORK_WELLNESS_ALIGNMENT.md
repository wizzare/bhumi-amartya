# Innerwork Wellness Alignment

Audit date: 19 June 2026

## Low-domain tests

| Condition | Can high-effort content still appear? | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Low body | Yes. HIIT, 20–30 minute movement, and multiple selectable exercises remain visible. | FAIL |
| Low emotion | Yes. Deep journaling and emotionally loaded exercises are not filtered by capacity. | FAIL |
| Low relationship | Yes. Connection/vulnerability practices can still be offered without readiness checks. | FAIL |

Wellness currently influences the experience mainly by determining whether a recent assessment exists. Completing a scan does not mean the resulting scores constrain practice type, intensity, duration, or number of exercises.

The dormant profile adapter can choose high-intensity movement from Human Design authority alone. The old generator can mark difficulty as beginner when Astro is intense, but does not use low body/emotion/relationship scores. The active local engine returns no recommendation.

## Safety finding

A RECOVERY user can enter the same unrestricted module library as a GROWTH user and select every exercise on a page. No runtime guard caps duration or suppresses high-effort practices.

**FAIL**
