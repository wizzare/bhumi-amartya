# Catatan V6 Closing Quality Audit

## New Closing Contract

The final closing answers:

> If Bhumi could leave only one message today, what would it be?

## Narrative Shape

The closing is written as one continuous message:

1. Names the active issue.
2. Removes shame and excessive self-pressure.
3. Interprets yesterday when history exists.
4. Uses profile only to explain why the pattern may strengthen.
5. Uses Astro only as a possible amplifier, never a cause.
6. Returns to the issue’s growth opportunity.
7. Leaves one compassionate direction.

## Length

The closing is sentence-limited to a maximum of 180 words and designed to remain within the requested 120–180-word range.

## Forbidden Patterns

- No bullet summary
- No challenge/opportunity variable dump
- No raw Astro statement
- No profile-report ending
- No generic coaching checklist

**PASS**
