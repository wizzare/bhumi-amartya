## Day Context

All seven day themes are implemented:

- Senin: beginning, intention, direction
- Selasa: rhythm and consistency
- Rabu: process and learning
- Kamis: perspective and meaning
- Jumat: weekly reflection and appreciation
- Sabtu: breathing room and personal space
- Minggu: cycle closure and preparation
