# TRANSLATION DICTIONARY LAYER V1

## OVERVIEW
The Translation Dictionary Layer acts as the critical bridge between the `CanonicalTranslatorService` and the user-facing platforms (Profile, Dashboard, Gaia). Its sole purpose is to intercept raw esoteric variables (e.g., "Arcana 8", "Rahu", "4/6 Profile") and convert them into deeply resonant, system-agnostic human narratives.

The guiding rule of this layer: **No system jargon. No numbers. No codes.** 

---

## PART 1: DICTIONARY INVENTORY

The following esoteric outputs currently bleed through the translator and must be intercepted by this dictionary layer:

**Astrology & Vedic:**
- Zodiac Signs (Aries, Taurus, etc.)
- Planetary Names (Mars, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu, Pluto, Chiron, Lilith)
- Dashas (Mahadasha, Antardasha)
- House Numbers (House 1, House 12)

**Human Design:**

**Destiny Matrix:**
- Arcana Numbers (1 through 22)
- Karmic Tails (e.g., 18-6-15, 9-3-21)
- Talent / Love / Money Line positions

**Numerology & BaZi:**
- Master Numbers (11, 22, 33)
- Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water)

## PART 7: NARRATIVE GENERATION RULES

To ensure consistency, the translation layer must strictly enforce these rules before outputting to the UI:

1. **Rule of Human Translation:** For every variable `X` mapped from the database, the string must answer: *"What does this mean for the user in real life?"*
2. **Rule of Zero Jargon:** Do not say "Kamu adalah Projector". Say, "Sistem energimu dirancang untuk memandu orang lain, bukan untuk bekerja keras layaknya mesin kuda."
3. **Rule of Actionability:** Every shadow or block translation must be paired with an exit route. Do not leave the user with just the wound; provide the band-aid.
4. **Rule of Empathy:** Never frame a shadow or block as a "fault" or "curse." Frame it as a "protective mechanism" that has overstayed its welcome.

## PART 8: READABILITY TEST

Before any translated dictionary string is pushed to production, it must pass the **3-Tier Generational Test**:

- **Tier 1: Age 17 (The Seeker)**
  - *Question*: Is the text relatable enough that a teenager can map it to their high school friendships, crushes, and identity crises?
  - *Fails if*: It sounds too bureaucratic or corporate.

- **Tier 2: Age 35 (The Builder)**
  - *Question*: Is the text actionable enough that a mid-career professional can apply it to their workplace stress, marriage, and financial goals?
  - *Fails if*: It sounds too mystical or impractical ("You must align with the cosmos").

- **Tier 3: Age 60 (The Reflector)**
  - *Question*: Is the text respectful and deep enough that a senior citizen can find peace and validation for their life choices?
  - *Fails if*: It uses overly trendy internet slang or dismissive tone. 

**Standard:** If a single output string passes all three tiers without requiring the user to Google a spiritual term, the dictionary entry is verified and ready for `CanonicalIdentity`.
