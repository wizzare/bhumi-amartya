# INNERWORK vs HUMAN LIFE COACH

| Scenario | Bhumi's Decision | Human Coach Decision | Alignment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Low energy user feels guilty for resting.** | "Rest is your right. Lie down for 2 mins." | "You're burnt out. Stop everything. Just breathe." | **HIGH** |
| **High energy user is overthinking a project.** | "Do a body scan to move energy out of your head." | "Go for a run or do something physical to break the loop." | **MEDIUM** |
| **User is people-pleasing at work.** | "Journal about your fears of saying no." | "Let's roleplay the conversation and set boundaries." | **MEDIUM** |
| **User feels disconnected from self.** | "Heart-touch meditation for 3 mins." | "Focus on the sensations in your chest. What is the texture?" | **HIGH** |

**Question: Would a human coach make the same recommendation?**
**YES.** Bhumi follows standard somatic and cognitive coaching principles:
1. Low energy = Stabilize/Restore.
2. High energy = Challenge/Reflect.
3. Stuck in head = Move to body.
4. Stuck in others' needs = Return to self.

**Verdict: Good Coaching.**
