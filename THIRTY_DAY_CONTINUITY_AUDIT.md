# Thirty-Day Continuity Audit

## Simulation

Thirty Journey records were executed through the actual learning engines with:

`Over Responsibility → Boundary → Self Worth → Rest`

## Generated evolution

Growth Narrative preserved the transition sequence:

```text
Over Responsibility
↓
Boundary Issues
↓
Self Worth
↓
Difficulty Resting
```

Generated layers also changed:

- Weekly Learning reflected the newest Rest records.
- Monthly Theme selected the most frequent category.
- Coach Memory compared body-based and writing practices.
- Practice Effectiveness calculated scores from reflection results.

## Runtime reach

These outputs are recomputed on read. They are not stored as learned state and are shown only in Journey detail.

Tomorrow’s Innerwork receives raw last-30 records and can avoid recent practices or react to helpful/heavy categories.

Tomorrow’s Dashboard/Catatan does not receive the generated weekly, monthly, growth, coach, or effectiveness outputs.

## Answer

Bhumi does more than merely store: it derives learning summaries and limited Innerwork adaptation.

Bhumi does not yet evolve across the complete companion loop because learned summaries do not return to Dashboard/Catatan and there is no durable progression level.
