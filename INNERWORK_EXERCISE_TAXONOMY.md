## Taxonomy gaps

- Almost no native 2-minute variants.
- Forgiveness and grief coverage is thin.
- Several audio descriptions contain unsupported technical or medical-style claims and require language hardening before V5 use.
- High-intensity movement must be excluded from RECOVERY.
- Manifestation exercises are action-oriented and should not be the primary practice when the user first needs regulation or emotional processing.
