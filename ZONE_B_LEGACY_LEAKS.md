# Zone B Legacy Leaks

## Recommendations/content unchanged when Zone A changes

### Journaling

The selected page theme is driven by date, Life Path, Arcana, Human Design type, and previous-entry count. Its entire theme bank can remain unchanged when Zone A changes.

### Meditation, Breathwork, and Mudra

Meditation theme is driven by Unified Blueprint Synthesis, date, and history. Breathwork and Mudra reuse that page. Zone A is absent.

### Yoga

All entries in `YOGA_DATABASE` always appear:

- Earth Connection Yoga
- Heart Opening Flow
- Warrior Confidence
- Vocal Clarity Flow
- Sacral Fluidity
- Crown Silence Flow

### Workout

All `WORKOUT_DATABASE` and workout variation entries always appear. Workout is not part of `supportPractices`.

### Audio Healing

The same embedded playlist appears for every issue.

### Static exploration hub

Journaling, Meditation, Audio Healing, Manifestasi, Workout, Yoga, and Herbal tiles always appear in the accordion regardless of Zone A.

## Classification

- Issue-aware: supporting card title/reason only.
- Legacy/static leak: destination content and exploration inventory.
