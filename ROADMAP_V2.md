## 2. V2 Backlog & Requirements
- **Daily Completion Logic**: 2 activities from ANY of the 6 pillars (Journaling, Meditation, Audio, Workout, Yoga, Herbal).
- **Completion Levels**:
    - 2 Activities: **Daily Completion** (Unlocks Journey)
    - 4 Activities: **Deep Practice**
    - 6 Activities: **Full Integration**
- **Tester Feedback (Mandatory Questions)**:
    - Apakah login lancar?
    - Apakah Catatan Hari Ini terasa personal?
    - Apakah alasan "Mengapa" mudah dipahami?
    - Apakah Innerwork tidak terasa membebani?
    - Apakah Journey terbuka setelah 2 aktivitas?
    - **PENTING**: "Apakah kamu ingin membuka Bhumi lagi besok pagi?"
    - Apakah ada bug, loading lama, atau tampilan aneh?

## 3. Alpha Testing Phase (v1.1.7)
- **Objective**: Collect 14 days of data from 20-50 testers.
- **Goal**: Validate if Daily Guidance V2 feels "personal and mentor-like" rather than a generic horoscope.
- **Freeze**: No major engine changes until feedback is processed.
