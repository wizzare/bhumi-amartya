# Mirror V4 Personalization Audit

## Blind Identification Test

Question: Can a reader distinguish users without seeing their names?

| User | Distinguishable? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Widhi | Partially | Stable-anchor identity paragraph |
| Ning | Partially | Order-from-chaos identity paragraph |
| Widya | No | Identical to Amartya |
| Amartya | No | Identical to Widya |
| Eva | Partially | Notices overlooked details |

## Personalization Depth

Only one paragraph changes by user. That paragraph does not change by day.

The day context is shared globally, while the personal paragraph is appended without synthesis. The result feels assembled from two separate blocks rather than written for a person in a particular moment.

## Problematic Claims

Some lines are absolute rather than reflective:

- “Orang lain mencari ketenangan dari kehadiranmu...”
- “Pikiranmu bekerja lebih cepat dari orang kebanyakan...”
- “Kamu selalu memiliki dorongan...”

These statements risk sounding like profile-report conclusions.

## Verdict

**FAIL**

Three users have some recognizable distinction, but Widya and Amartya are impossible to distinguish without their names.
