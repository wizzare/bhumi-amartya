# Bhumi Amartya - Installation Guide

Follow these steps to install the app on your physical Android device.

## Step 1: Enable "Install Unknown Apps"
On your Android phone:
1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Search for **"Install unknown apps"**.
3. Select the browser or file manager you will use to open the APK (e.g., Chrome or Files).
4. Toggle on **"Allow from this source"**.

## Step 2: Transfer APK to phone
1. Connect your phone to your computer via USB OR use a cloud service (Google Drive, Telegram, etc.).
2. Copy `bhumi-amartya-internal-test-v0.1.apk` to your phone's storage.

## Step 3: Install APK
1. Open the file manager on your phone.
2. Locate the APK file.
3. Tap on it and select **Install**.
4. If prompted by Play Protect, select **"Install anyway"** (it is a debug build).

## Step 4: Open Bhumi Amartya
1. Find the **Bhumi Amartya** icon in your app drawer.
2. Tap to launch.

## Step 5: Register/Login
1. Tap **"Mulai Perjalanan"** to register or **"Saya Sudah Punya Akun"** to login.
2. Follow the Google Auth flow.

## Step 6: Complete onboarding
1. Enter your birth data accurately.
2. Wait for the blueprint generation.

## Step 7: Verify
*   **Blueprint:** Ensure Life Path, Human Design, and Arcana Center are correct.
*   **Refleksi Jiwa:** Verify the daily reflection text is visible and meaningful.
*   **Catatan Hari Ini:** Verify the daily note includes planetary context.
*   **Journal:** Create an entry, save it, and ensure it persists after app restart.
