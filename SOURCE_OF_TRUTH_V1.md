# BHUMI SOURCE OF TRUTH V1

## OVERVIEW

Bhumi has evolved from a collection of isolated astrological and esoteric blueprints into a unified **Personal Intelligence Platform**. With 8 complete systems (Numerology, Human Design, Natal Chart, Weton, BaZi, Vedic, Destiny Matrix, Tzolkin) generating rich data, the architectural challenge transitions from data creation to data harmonization. 

This document defines the **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)** and **Canonical Data Model** for Bhumi. It aggregates information from multiple systems of record into one unified view, ensuring that each source system remains authoritative for its specific domain while contributing to a shared, holistic human identity.

---

## PART 1: SYSTEM OF RECORD AUDIT

Each of the 8 systems serves as a System of Record for specific aspects of the human experience.

### 1. Numerology
- **Primary Domain**: Foundational Purpose & Core Essence
- **Secondary Domain**: Inherent Traits
- **Strength**: High-level, easily understood life path mapping.
- **Weakness**: Lacks granular timing and physical/energetic specificity.
- **Should Own**: The "why" of the soul's journey (Life Path).
- **Should NOT Own**: Daily timing, physical energy mechanics.

### 2. Human Design
- **Primary Domain**: Energetic Mechanics & Decision Making
- **Secondary Domain**: Biological/Physical Alignment (Variables)
- **Strength**: Unmatched precision in aura mechanics, strategy, and environmental alignment.
- **Weakness**: Complex vocabulary; lacks long-term chronological narrative.
- **Should Own**: Action logic (Strategy/Authority), energetic boundaries.
- **Should NOT Own**: Past life karma, spiritual archetypes.

### 3. Natal Chart (Western Astrology)
- **Primary Domain**: Psychological Makeup & Core Ego
- **Secondary Domain**: Emotional processing and physical appearance (Ascendant)
- **Strength**: Deep psychological nuance and structural personality mapping.
- **Weakness**: Can become overly deterministic or vague in complex aspect geometries.
- **Should Own**: Emotional needs (Moon), ego expression (Sun), and psychological geometry.
- **Should NOT Own**: Absolute career paths, exact daily energy levels.

### 4. Weton
- **Primary Domain**: Cultural Chronology & Character (Watak)
- **Secondary Domain**: Traditional life missions
- **Strength**: Deeply rooted in Javanese cultural rhythms and social harmony.
- **Weakness**: Redundant in career/relationship domains compared to modern models.
- **Should Own**: Character traits specific to cultural grounding (Watak).
- **Should NOT Own**: Modern career paths, complex psychological trauma mapping.

### 5. BaZi (Four Pillars)
- **Primary Domain**: Elemental Balance & Chronological Luck Cycles
- **Secondary Domain**: Career potential and inherent strengths
- **Strength**: Precise elemental mapping and 10-year macroeconomic/luck cycles.
- **Weakness**: Rigid societal framing around "luck" and "favorable/unfavorable".
- **Should Own**: Elemental constitution and macro-timing (Luck Cycles).
- **Should NOT Own**: Emotional psychology, specific relationship dynamics.

### 6. Vedic Astrology
- **Primary Domain**: Karmic Imprints & Spiritual Evolution
- **Secondary Domain**: Long-term chronological periods (Dashas)
- **Strength**: Deepest mapping of soul-level karma (Nodes/Atmakaraka) and exact period timing.
- **Weakness**: Highly fatalistic traditional vocabulary.
- **Should Own**: Soul-level spiritual karma and major life chapters (Dashas).
- **Should NOT Own**: Daily energetic mechanics, modern psychological framing.

### 7. Destiny Matrix
- **Primary Domain**: Psychological Patterns, Relationships & Wealth
- **Secondary Domain**: Health/Chakra mapping and ancestral karma
- **Strength**: Highly actionable, deeply structural mapping of the subconscious, love, and money lines.
- **Weakness**: Can be overly complex in its numerological derivations.
- **Should Own**: Repeated patterns, relationship/money dynamics, inner child.
- **Should NOT Own**: Elemental balance, day-to-day decision authority.

### 8. Tzolkin
- **Primary Domain**: Galactic Timing & Archetypal Growth
- **Secondary Domain**: Life purpose and relationship dynamics
- **Strength**: Synchronistic daily tracking and poetic archetypes (Kin).
- **Weakness**: Highly abstract and esoteric.
- **Should Own**: Daily synchronicity and archetypal growth rhythms.
- **Should NOT Own**: Actionable psychological trauma, specific career roles.

## PART 2: FIELD OWNERSHIP

To prevent narrative collision, we establish a rigid hierarchy of truth for the Canonical Identity Model.

### Purpose
- **Primary Owner**: Numerology (Life Path)
- **Secondary**: Destiny Matrix (Center/Destiny Point)
- **Support**: Tzolkin (Wavespell)
- **Conflict Resolution**: Numerology sets the broad theme; Destiny Matrix provides the specific actions required to achieve it.

### Identity
- **Primary Owner**: Natal Chart (Sun/Moon/Ascendant)
- **Secondary**: Human Design (Profile)
- **Support**: Weton (Watak)
- **Conflict Resolution**: Natal Chart defines the *what* (the personality), Human Design defines the *how* (the archetype).

### Energy
- **Primary Owner**: Human Design (Type, Centers)
- **Secondary**: BaZi (Day Master, Elemental Balance)
- **Support**: Destiny Matrix (Health Chart/Physics)
- **Conflict Resolution**: HD defines the aura mechanic; BaZi defines the elemental constitution feeding that aura.

### Psychology
- **Primary Owner**: Natal Chart (Planetary aspects, Moon)
- **Secondary**: Destiny Matrix (Chart Heart)
- **Support**: Numerology (Soul Urge)
- **Conflict Resolution**: Natal Chart defines emotional processing; Destiny Matrix defines subconscious fears.

### Archetype
- **Primary Owner**: Human Design (Incarnation Cross / Profile)
- **Secondary**: Tzolkin (Kin / Solar Seal)
- **Support**: Destiny Matrix (Arcana Center)
- **Conflict Resolution**: HD acts as the primary role; Tzolkin adds the mythic/poetic flavor.

### Shadow
- **Primary Owner**: Destiny Matrix (Karmic Tail / Inner Child)
- **Secondary**: Natal Chart (Lilith / Pluto)
- **Support**: Tzolkin (Galactic Tone Shadow)
- **Conflict Resolution**: Destiny Matrix is the actionable psychological shadow; Natal Chart is the deep, structural, unconscious shadow.

### Karma
- **Primary Owner**: Vedic (Atmakaraka / Rahu & Ketu)
- **Secondary**: Destiny Matrix (Ancestor Lines)
- **Support**: Natal Chart (Lunar Nodes)
- **Conflict Resolution**: Vedic owns the overarching soul karma; Matrix owns immediate ancestral karma.

### Talents
- **Primary Owner**: Destiny Matrix (Talents Line: Father/Mother/Personal)
- **Secondary**: Human Design (Defined Channels / Gates)
- **Support**: BaZi (Ten Gods Strengths)
- **Conflict Resolution**: Matrix defines the expressed talent; HD defines the energetic consistency of that talent.

### Relationships
- **Primary Owner**: Destiny Matrix (Love Line)
- **Secondary**: Vedic (Darakaraka / 7th House)
- **Support**: Natal Chart (Venus / Mars)
- **Conflict Resolution**: Matrix defines the actionable relationship patterns; Vedic/Natal define inherent needs and attractions.

### Career
- **Primary Owner**: Destiny Matrix (Money Line)
- **Secondary**: BaZi (Ten Gods / Career Path)
- **Support**: Natal Chart (Midheaven / 10th House)
- **Conflict Resolution**: Matrix defines how wealth flows; BaZi defines the industry/elemental fit.

### Health
- **Primary Owner**: Destiny Matrix (Health Chart / Chakra Matrix)
- **Secondary**: Human Design (Variables: Digestion / Environment)
- **Support**: BaZi (Elemental Imbalances)
- **Conflict Resolution**: Matrix defines the psychological-physical link (Chakras); HD defines the biological interface (Digestion/Environment).

### Spirituality
- **Primary Owner**: Vedic (Moksha Focus / 9th & 12th House)
- **Secondary**: Tzolkin (Castle / Spiritual Lesson)
- **Support**: Destiny Matrix (Spiritual Purpose)
- **Conflict Resolution**: Vedic defines the path to liberation; Tzolkin defines the daily synchronicity of the spiritual path.

### Growth
- **Primary Owner**: Innerwork/Journey Engine (Dynamic user data)
- **Secondary**: Destiny Matrix (Yearly Arcana)
- **Support**: Tzolkin (Wavespell Growth Direction)
- **Conflict Resolution**: Active user actions (Innerwork) ALWAYS override static blueprint predictions.

### Timing
- **Primary Owner**: Vedic (Dashas) - *Macro Timing*
- **Secondary**: BaZi (Luck Pillars) - *Macro Timing*
- **Support**: Tzolkin (Daily Kin) - *Micro Timing*
- **Conflict Resolution**: Dashas and Luck Pillars set the decade-long themes; Tzolkin sets the daily mood.

## PART 3: CANONICAL IDENTITY MODEL

The `CanonicalIdentity` object serves as the universal interface for all Bhumi systems.

```typescript
interface CanonicalIdentity {
  purpose: {
    theme: string;           // Derived from Numerology (Life Path)
    action: string;          // Derived from Destiny Matrix (Destiny Point)
    flavor: string;          // Derived from Tzolkin (Wavespell)
  };
  identity: {
    coreEgo: string;         // Derived from Natal (Sun/Ascendant)
    archetypeRole: string;   // Derived from HD (Profile)
    culturalGrounding: string; // Derived from Weton (Watak)
  };
  energy: {
    mechanic: string;        // Derived from HD (Type/Strategy)
    constitution: string;    // Derived from BaZi (Elements)
    vitality: number;        // Derived from Destiny Matrix (Physics)
  };
  psychology: {
    emotionalNeeds: string;  // Derived from Natal (Moon)
    subconsciousDrive: string; // Derived from Numerology (Soul Urge)
  };
  archetype: {
    primary: string;         // Derived from HD (Incarnation Cross)
    mythic: string;          // Derived from Tzolkin (Kin)
  };
  shadow: {
    actionablePatterns: string; // Derived from Destiny Matrix (Karmic Tail)
    deepWounds: string;         // Derived from Natal (Lilith/Chiron)
  };
  karma: {
    soulLesson: string;      // Derived from Vedic (Atmakaraka/Nodes)
    ancestral: string;       // Derived from Destiny Matrix (Ancestor Line)
  };
  talents: {
    manifested: string[];    // Derived from Destiny Matrix (Talents)
    consistent: string[];    // Derived from HD (Channels)
  };
  relationships: {
    patterns: string;        // Derived from Destiny Matrix (Love Line)
    needs: string;           // Derived from Natal/Vedic (Venus/Darakaraka)
  };
  career: {
    wealthFlow: string;      // Derived from Destiny Matrix (Money Line)
    environmentFit: string;  // Derived from BaZi (Ten Gods)
  };
  health: {
    chakraState: any;        // Derived from Destiny Matrix (Chakra Matrix)
    biologicalInterface: any;// Derived from HD (Variables)
  };
  spirituality: {
    path: string;            // Derived from Vedic (Moksha)
    dailySynchronicity: string; // Derived from Tzolkin
  };
  growth: {
    currentStage: string;    // Derived from Active Innerwork Data
    yearlyTheme: string;     // Derived from Destiny Matrix (Yearly Arcana)
  };
  timing: {
    macroCycle: string;      // Derived from Vedic (Dasha) / BaZi (Luck Pillar)
    microDaily: string;      // Derived from Tzolkin
  };
}
```

## PART 4: PROFILE MAPPING (PROFILE V4)

Profile V4 transitions from "System Tabs" (e.g., Destiny Matrix, HD) to "Identity Tabs" (e.g., Energy, Purpose, Shadow).

**Flow:** Raw Systems ➔ `insightTranslator` ➔ `CanonicalIdentity` ➔ UI Component

- **Identity Section**: Consumes `CanonicalIdentity.identity`, `.archetype`, `.purpose`.
- **Peta Jiwa (Soul Map) Section**: Consumes `CanonicalIdentity.psychology`, `.shadow`, `.karma`.
- **Potensi (Potentials) Section**: Consumes `CanonicalIdentity.talents`, `.career`, `.relationships`.
- **Kondisi (State/Energy) Section**: Consumes `CanonicalIdentity.energy`, `.health`.
- **Pertumbuhan (Growth) Section**: Consumes `CanonicalIdentity.growth`, `.timing`.

## PART 5: DASHBOARD MAPPING

The Dashboard consumes Canonical domains directly, abstracting the source systems away from the user.

- **Refleksi Jiwa**: Feeds on `growth.currentStage` + `timing.microDaily`.
- **Saran Bhumi**: Feeds on `energy.mechanic` + `shadow.actionablePatterns`.
- **Catatan Hari Ini**: Feeds on `timing.microDaily` (Tzolkin) + `growth.yearlyTheme`.
- **Astro Hari Ini**: Feeds on live astrological transits mapped against `identity.coreEgo` (Natal).
- **Journey**: Feeds on `karma.soulLesson` + `shadow.deepWounds`.
- **Share Cards**: Selects striking highlights from `archetype.primary`, `purpose.theme`, or `talents.manifested`.
- **Daily Guidance**: Feeds on `energy.mechanic` (HD Strategy) + `timing.microDaily`.

- **Healthy Food**: Driven by `health.biologicalInterface` (HD Digestion Variables) and `energy.constitution` (BaZi Elements).

## PART 7: GAIA MAPPING

Gaia V4 represents a paradigm shift. Gaia no longer parses raw `blueprint.humanDesign.type` or `blueprint.destinyMatrix.center`.

**Current Implementation (V3):**
Gaia reads raw blueprint structures and acts as an astrologer/reader interpreting the systems.

**Future Implementation (V4):**
Gaia consumes the **Canonical Identity Model**. 
Gaia does not say: *"Sebagai Generator dengan Life Path 1..."*
Gaia says: *"Dengan energi bawaanmu yang dirancang untuk merespons, dan tujuan utamamu untuk merintis jalan baru..."*

Gaia System Prompt injection:
```json
{
  "Identity": CanonicalIdentity.identity,
  "CurrentEnergy": CanonicalIdentity.energy,
  "ActiveShadow": CanonicalIdentity.shadow.actionablePatterns,
  "CurrentTiming": CanonicalIdentity.timing.macroCycle
}
```
This forces Gaia to act as a holistic intelligence entity rather than an aggregate calculator, drastically reducing prompt token sizes and eliminating conflicting system advice (e.g., BaZi predicting bad luck while Tzolkin predicts a perfect day).

## PART 8: GAP ANALYSIS

- **Duplicate Domains**: Career and Relationship advice is currently generated independently by BaZi, Vedic, Matrix, and Weton. **Action**: Silence Weton/BaZi/Vedic career derivations in the UI in favor of Destiny Matrix to prevent contradiction.
- **Conflicting Domains**: Vedic Dashas vs BaZi Luck Pillars. Both dictate macro-timing but use entirely different frameworks. **Action**: Merge their insights into a generalized "Current Macro Theme" rather than exposing the specific contradictory dates.
- **Unused Data**: HD Variables (Digestion, Cognition) and Vedic Yogas are heavily calculated but entirely unmapped in the current Canonical model proposals. **Action**: Introduce them specifically into Innerwork (Diet/Movement for HD Variables; Meditation for Yogas).
- **Hidden Data**: Destiny Matrix Health Chart numbers. **Action**: Map directly to the `health.chakraState` canonical field.
- **Dead Data**: Weton's Work/Money style. **Action**: Deprecate in UI; keep in backend for legacy or cultural share cards only.
- **Future Opportunities**: The Canonical Identity model allows for simple insertion of new engines (e.g., Enneagram, MBTI) by simply mapping them to `psychology` or `archetype` domains without refactoring the entire frontend.

## PART 9: ROADMAP

### Phase 1: Source Of Truth Definition (Current)
- Establish the ownership matrix.
- Align the engineering and product teams on the SSOT principles.
- **Dependency**: None.

### Phase 2: Canonical Identity Layer (Backend)
- Create `CanonicalIdentity` TypeScript interfaces.
- Build `CanonicalTranslatorService` to sit between the raw Blueprint DB and the frontend/AI.
- **Risk**: Heavy translation logic could introduce latency.
- **Migration**: Run parallel to V3. Do not replace the raw `blueprint` object yet; create a derived `canonicalProfile` node.

### Phase 3: Profile V4 (Frontend)
- Refactor `ProfileTabs.tsx` to consume `CanonicalIdentity`.
- Shift from System-based UI (HD Tab, Matrix Tab) to Domain-based UI (Energy Tab, Potentials Tab).
- **Dependency**: Phase 2 completion.
- **Risk**: Users attached to specific system terminology (e.g., "I want to see my Human Design chart") may feel alienated. **Mitigation**: Retain "Raw Chart" visual components (Bodygraph, Matrix graph) as deeply nested views.

### Phase 4: Dashboard V4 (Frontend)
- Reroute Dashboard components to pull from `CanonicalIdentity`.
- Ensure daily summaries merge Macro and Micro timing domains safely.
- **Dependency**: Phase 2.

### Phase 5: Innerwork Intelligence
- Tag all Innerwork content (meditations, journals) with Canonical Domain tags (e.g., `health.chakraState: Root`, `energy.mechanic: Generator`).
- Build the recommendation engine.
- **Dependency**: Phase 2 and a content tagging effort.

### Phase 6: Gaia V4
- Rewrite Gaia's system prompts to accept `CanonicalIdentity` JSON rather than raw blueprint stringified objects.
- Train Gaia to speak holistically rather than system-specifically.
- **Risk**: Gaia might lose esoteric nuance if the canonical translation is too vague. **Mitigation**: Pass specific `CanonicalIdentity` fields *and* the raw `blueprint` as a fallback context, but instruct Gaia to prioritize the canonical interpretation.
- **Dependency**: All prior phases.
