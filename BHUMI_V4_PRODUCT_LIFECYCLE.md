# BHUMI V4 PRODUCT LIFECYCLE

**Authority:** [BHUMI_V4_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md](BHUMI_V4_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md)

---

## 1. VISION & STRATEGY
All product features must align with the "Living Intelligence" vision. New concepts are vetted against Product Principles (Companion First, Identity First, Memory First, Human Meaning First).

---

## 2. ROADMAP & PHASES
The roadmap is divided into strategic Phases (A-G). Progress is tracked via Sprints, and Phase Exits are governed by strict technical and product criteria.

---

## 3. FEATURE LIFECYCLE
1.  **Conception:** Ideation and alignment with vision.
2.  **RFC:** Formal request for change and Founder review.
3.  **Design:** Architecture and UI/UX specification.
4.  **Implementation:** Development following Developer Rules.
5.  **Testing:** Comprehensive QA and regression checks.
6.  **Release:** Staged rollout and monitoring.
7.  **Maintenance:** Ongoing bug fixes and performance tuning.

---

## 4. DEPRECATION POLICY
Legacy features or modules are deprecated only when replaced by a synchronized V4 equivalent that maintains full data parity.
