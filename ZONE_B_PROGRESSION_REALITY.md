# Zone B Progression Reality

## Practice effectiveness simulation

Scenario 1:

- Practice type A, helpful ×10.
- Practice type B, heavy ×10.

Scenario 2 reversed the helpful/heavy assignments.

Both scenarios produced the same six support recommendations.

## Why weighting has no effect

The engine assigns:

- Helpful category: +3.
- Heavy category: −5.
- Recently used ID: −20.

Each issue/category has only one support candidate. Sorting a one-item list cannot change selection.

## Thirty-day simulation

Day 1 main recommendation:

`difficulty-resting-honest-reflection`

Day 30 main recommendation:

`difficulty-resting-honest-reflection`

Day 1 and Day 30 support recommendations were exactly identical.

Day 30 only added Journey entries to `sourceSignals`.

## Verdict

- Variation: yes.
- Short-memory repetition avoidance: yes.
- Outcome-based weighting with visible effect: no.
- Stage progression: no.
- Day 1 → Day 30 curriculum: no.
