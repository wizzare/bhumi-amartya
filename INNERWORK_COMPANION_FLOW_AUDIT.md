# Innerwork Companion Flow Audit

Audit date: 19 June 2026

## Final verdict

# FAIL

Innerwork is not yet the dependable next logical step after Catatan Hari Ini.

## Why

1. Catatan derives one dominant issue inside `DailyNoteV2`, but that issue does not enter the Innerwork runtime.
2. Navigator mode is completely bypassed by the Innerwork hub.
3. Wellness completion gates visibility, but wellness scores do not control effort or practice selection.
4. Journey activities are stored and may enter the AI prompt, but completion history does not reliably rotate or continue practices.
5. The active local recommendation engine returns an empty object.
6. The static library remains the most reliable rendered experience, and it is the same broad catalog for every user.
7. Alternative profile and Life Path generators exist, but they are dormant or legacy paths rather than one authoritative decision engine.
